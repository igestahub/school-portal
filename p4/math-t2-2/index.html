Assessment Guidelines & Visual Rules

1. 3D Nets & Spatial Folding

Inspect the CSS Grid net layouts carefully. Opposite faces on a cube never share an edge or corner when folded.

2. Angle Rules & Diagrams

Angle diagrams are not drawn to physical protractor scale unless on the Protractor Reading slide. Use geometric angle rules (90°, 180°, 360°) to calculate exact answers.

3. Fractions & Simplest Form

Write fractions using a slash like 3/8 and mixed numbers with a space like 2 1/4. Zero hints are shown during the test.
